Fish With Do

Steer the maze ball on your computer with your phone's accelerometer. No backend, peer-to-peer over WebRTC.

Phone → Controller Open on your iPhone Computer → Game Open on your computer
  1. Open the Game page on your computer — a QR code appears automatically.
  2. Scan it with your phone's camera — the controller page opens by itself.
  3. Tap Enable motion, then copy the answer the phone shows.
  4. Paste the answer into the computer — it connects automatically. Tilt to steer to the green goal.
